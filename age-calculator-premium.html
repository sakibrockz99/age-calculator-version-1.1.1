How the counting works

Years count completed birthdays using your chosen February 29 rule. Months are counted forward from the last birthday and clamp to shorter month ends; remaining days are counted after that.

Total months are counted independently from your birth date. Dates use the Gregorian calendar, from year 0001 through 9999. Fixed dates refer to the start of that date.

Calendar-equivalent hours, minutes and seconds use 24 hours per day. They are not measured lifetime durations: birth time, birth timezone and daylight-saving changes are not included. “Today” adds your local clock time.

For examination or employment eligibility, use the exact cutoff date and the authority’s own rules. This is not a legal age determination.
